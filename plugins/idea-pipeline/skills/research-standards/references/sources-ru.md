# Source catalog: Russia and CIS

Starting points, not a limit. Check that a page is reachable and current before relying on it. Sources marked **user export** need an account or a paid plan: list them under Data requests instead of guessing.

## Statistics and registries

| Source | What it gives | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Rosstat (rosstat.gov.ru) | Population, households, incomes, industries, number of organizations | Free |
| EMISS (fedstat.ru) | Official statistical indicators by region and period | Free |
| Bank of Russia (cbr.ru) | Payments, lending, financial market statistics | Free |
| GIR BO (bo.nalog.ru) | Annual financial statements of companies: revenue and profit of competitors | Free |
| FNS open data, EGRUL (nalog.gov.ru) | Registered companies by activity code (OKVED), SME register | Free |
| Rusprofile, List-org | Company cards with revenue, OKVED, founders | Free, partly |
| Kontur.Focus, SPARK-Interfax | Company financials, affiliations, court cases, industry samples | **User export** |
| EIS public procurement (zakupki.gov.ru) | What the state and state companies buy, at what price | Free |
| Register of Russian software (reestr.digital.gov.ru) | Registered software products by class: competitors in B2B and B2G | Free |
| FIPS (fips.ru) | Patents, trademarks, software registrations | Free |

## Notes

- Revenue from GIR BO covers the legal entity, which may run several businesses. Say so when you use it for a competitor's size.
- Regulations that often block or shape a product: 152-FZ (personal data, data localization), 54-FZ (online cash registers), licensing in finance, medicine and education, marketing rules for advertising. Check the ones that apply in the pre-flight.
