# Source catalog: global, US, EU and other markets

Starting points, not a limit. Check that a page is reachable and current before relying on it. Sources marked **user export** need an account or a paid plan: list them under Data requests instead of guessing.

## Statistics and registries

| Source | What it gives | Access |
|---|---|---|
| World Bank Open Data, OECD Data | Country-level population, income, industry and digital adoption indicators | Free |
| Eurostat | EU statistics: enterprises by size and sector, household spending, ICT use | Free |
| US Census Bureau (County Business Patterns, SUSB, ACS) | Number of businesses by industry and size, demographics | Free |
| US Bureau of Labor Statistics | Employment and wages by occupation: cost of doing the job by hand | Free |
| SEC EDGAR | 10-K and S-1 filings: revenue, segments, customer counts of public competitors | Free |
| UK Companies House; national business registers | Company filings and accounts | Free |
| Google Patents, Espacenet | Patents and prior art | Free |

## Market research

| Source | What it gives | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Statista, Gartner, Forrester, IDC, Grand View Research and similar | Market size estimates | Mostly paid. Free summaries often hide the method: prefer the underlying source they cite, and treat unexplained figures as type `vendor` |
| Industry associations and regulators | Industry sizes, member counts, rules | Free |

## Notes

- Market reports from research firms disagree with each other by a wide margin for new categories. Never use one as the only sizing method.
- Regulations that often shape a product: GDPR and the EU AI Act in the EU, sector rules (HIPAA, PCI DSS, financial licensing), consumer protection and subscription cancellation rules. Check the ones that apply in the pre-flight.
