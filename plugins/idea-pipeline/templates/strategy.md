## 1. Beachhead segment

## 2. Value proposition
Value Proposition Canvas for the beachhead: jobs, pains, gains, and how the product relieves and creates each. Then the value proposition in one sentence.

## 3. Positioning
Competitive alternatives → unique attributes → the value they enable → who cares most → market category. The positioning statement.

## 4. Competitive advantage
What would let us win and keep winning. Assess each source of lasting advantage (scale economies, network effects, counter-positioning, switching costs, brand, cornered resource, process power): present now, possible later, or absent, with evidence. Then the honest answer: what stops a funded competitor from copying this in six months.

Lean canvas. Revenue model and price hypotheses. Unit economics (price, gross margin, CAC by channel, retention, LTV, payback), every input labeled, as ranges.
