## Goal

## Who to talk to
Screener: the criteria that make someone a fit, and the ones that rule them out. Target number of interviews.

## Where to find them
Channels from `customers.md`, with a first message to send in each.

## Rules
- Talk about their life and past behavior, not about our idea.
- Ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future.
- Listen more than talk. No pitching until the end, if at all.
- Compliments and "I would buy it" are not evidence. Commitments are.
