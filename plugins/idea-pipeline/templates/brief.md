# <Idea name>: Brief

_Status: draft | confirmed · Date: YYYY-MM-DD_

## 1. The idea
One paragraph: what it is, for whom, and what changes for them. Then the idea in one sentence.

## 2. Problem and customers
- **Who has the problem**: the first segment you think of, as specifically as possible.
- **The problem**: in the customer's words, not the solution's.
- **What they do today**: current alternatives and workarounds, including doing nothing.
- **Why now**: what changed (technology, regulation, behavior, prices) that makes this possible or necessary now.

## 3. Market and model
- **Geography**, **languages**, **currency**.
- **Model**: B2B, B2C, B2B2C or B2G; who uses, who pays, who decides.
- **Revenue idea**: how money comes in, the price range in mind.

## 4. Us
- **Team**: who, competencies, time commitment.
- **Unfair advantages**: what we have that others cannot easily copy or buy: access, data, expertise, audience, distribution, relationships.
- **Founder–market fit**: why we would understand these customers better than others.
- **Resources and constraints**: budget, time, deadlines, things we will not do.

## 5. Ambition and kill conditions
- **Ambition**: lifestyle business or venture-scale.
- **Minimum outcome**: the revenue or result below which this is not worth our time.
- **Kill conditions**: what we would have to learn to stop, written now, before any research.

## 6. Readiness setup
- **Required axes**: CRL, BRL, TRL, TMRL by default; IPRL and FRL if they matter, and why.
- **Gate thresholds**: see `readiness.md` (proposed here, locked at the end of the first run).

## 7. Open questions
Anything deliberately left undecided.
