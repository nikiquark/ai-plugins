# Direction seeds

These are starting points for concepts, not templates. Each concept should combine **one direction** with **the subject's own world**. Propose at least one direction the user would not have thought of.

| Direction | Layout | Type | Color and texture | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Swiss / International** | A strict visible grid, flush-left ragged-right text, large numerals, asymmetric white space | Neo-grotesque or grotesque at extreme sizes, tight leading | Two colors plus black, flat | Precise slides along grid lines |
| **Brutalist / raw** | Exposed structure, borders as layout, default-looking controls used on purpose, dense | Mono plus a heavy grotesque, underlined links | Pure primaries or monochrome, no shadows | None, or abrupt state swaps |
| **Editorial magazine** | Multi-column, drop caps, pull quotes, image-led spreads, folios | A high-contrast serif display with a quiet text serif or sans | Paper tones and one ink accent, grain | Slow fades, scroll-linked image reveals |
| **Constructivist poster** | Diagonals, rotated type, overlapping blocks, big geometric shapes | Condensed heavy sans, all caps for display only | Red, black and off-white, halftone | Elements slide in on diagonals |
| **Retro print / risograph** | Misregistration, overprint, collage, stickers | A rounded or quirky display face plus a typewriter face | 2–3 spot colors, overprint blending, grain | Wobble, stamp-in |
| **Industrial / instrument** | Panels as on hardware, labels, rulers, readouts, gauges | Mono and DIN-like faces, tabular figures | Equipment greys, a warning orange or green readout | Counters ticking, needle sweeps |
| **Cartographic / blueprint** | Coordinates, legends, scale bars, layered plans | Technical sans, small-caps labels | Blueprint blue and white, or a topographic palette | Line-draw reveals |
| **Transit signage** | Wayfinding: big pictogram-free type, line colors, platform numbers | Signage grotesques | Line colors on dark or white, high contrast | Departure-board flips |
| **Scientific journal** | Figures, captions, numbered sections, footnotes, margins | A classic text serif plus a sans for figures | Restrained, one plot color | Minimal |
| **Receipt / ticket** | A narrow column, perforations, monospaced totals, stamps | Mono and dot-matrix display faces | Thermal-paper white, faded ink, a stamp red | Print-out feed |
| **Art deco geometric** | Symmetry, frames, sunbursts, stepped forms | Geometric display face with high contrast | Black, gold and a jewel tone | Elegant staggered reveals |
| **Luxury minimal** | Huge white space, a single image, tiny precise text | Light-weight serif display, wide tracking on the small text only | Monochrome plus one material tone | Very slow, refined fades |
| **Toy / playful** | Chunky shapes, bouncy stacking, oversized controls | Rounded heavy display face | Saturated primaries, flat | Springy physics |
| **Zine / collage** | Cut-out images, tape, handwritten annotations, irregular grid | A mix of 2 faces plus handwriting | Photocopy black and white plus one highlighter | Jitter, paper shuffles |

## How to present concepts

For each concept, write one sentence for the direction and one for the bold idea, for example "the pricing page is a ferry timetable". Then give the palette (hex values with roles), the type pairing with a reason, a hero, one content section, and one interactive element. Finally, state the trade-off honestly, for example "less scannable for returning users".
