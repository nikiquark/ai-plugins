# AI-look tells to avoid

Each item is a default that models reach for without thinking. Seeing two or three of them together is what makes a page read as "AI-generated".

## Layout
- A centered hero with a big headline, a grey subline, two buttons (primary plus ghost) and a screenshot below.
- A three- or four-column "features" grid of icon, bold title and two lines of text.
- A logo cloud of greyscale customer logos under the hero.
- A stats band ("10k+ users · 99.9% uptime · 24/7 support").
- Testimonial cards with circular avatars and five stars.
- Pricing as three cards with the middle one raised and badged "Most popular".
- An FAQ accordion followed by a giant gradient CTA box before the footer.
- Every section the same height, the same max-width and the same centered alignment.
- Bento grids of rounded tiles.

## Surfaces and decoration
- Rounded-2xl everything with a uniform soft shadow.
- Cards nested inside cards.
- Glassmorphism: frosted panels over a blurred colorful background.
- Glowing orbs, blurred blobs, aurora backgrounds.
- Neon glow shadows on buttons.
- Gradient borders around cards.
- Dot-grid or line-grid backgrounds used as filler, not as an expression of a real grid.
- Noise overlays applied by habit rather than to serve the direction.

## Typography
- Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Poppins and Montserrat (see the banned list).
- One accented word in the headline, in gradient or a different color.
- Tracked all-caps "eyebrow" labels above every heading.
- Middle-dot meta strings ("5 min read · Design · 2024").
- Arrows (→) appended to every link and button.
- Everything set at weights 400 and 600, with no real contrast.

## Color
- Purple or indigo gradients on white; blue-to-purple; pink-to-orange "sunset".
- Near-black with an acid-green or electric-lime accent (the "dev tool" look).
- Cream background with a terracotta accent (the "warm editorial" look).
- Slate greys with a single blue accent, which is Tailwind's default palette left untouched.

## Iconography and copy
- Lucide or heroicons outline icons beside every heading.
- Emoji as bullets or section markers (✨ 🚀 ⚡).
- Copy such as "Supercharge your workflow", "Built for the modern team", "Unlock the power of…" or "Seamless".
- Fake social proof and placeholder numbers.

## What to do instead
Pick the **subject's own vernacular**. A booking tool for a ferry company can borrow timetable boards, ticket stubs and maritime signal flags. A tax tool can borrow ledger paper and stamped forms. A music app can borrow record sleeves and setlists. The layout comes out of that world, and the clichés above stop being tempting because they don't fit.
